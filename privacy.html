Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Work Companion is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no user accounts, and the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What stays on your computer

  • Work log: plain markdown files in a folder you choose.
  • Settings: a settings file in the app's profile folder.
  • Secrets (AI API keys and Google sign-in tokens): stored encrypted in your operating system's keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain) or, on Linux, encrypted with your keyring. They are never stored in plaintext.

The app has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you sign in, Work Companion requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope and uses it only to:

  • list your calendars so you can pick which ones to follow;
  • read today's events (title, start and end time, and your response) to show them, alert you before meetings, and ask for a note when a meeting ends.

Event data is fetched directly from Google to your computer, kept in memory, and never sent to the developer or to any other party, including AI providers. The app never creates, edits or deletes calendar data. Signing out in the app revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Work Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI providers

AI features are optional. If you set up a provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or a custom server), the app sends the log entries needed for the feature you use, such as a standup summary or a question about your log, directly from your computer to that provider with your own key. That provider's privacy policy applies to those requests. With a local Ollama model, nothing leaves your computer.

Children

Work Companion is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
