Work Companion

A private desktop work companion for Windows, macOS and Linux. A small 3D character lives on your desktop and helps you through the workday: break and water reminders, a plain-markdown work log, a focus timer, meeting alerts from Google Calendar, and optional AI standup summaries.

Private by default

Your log is a set of markdown files on your own disk. Work Companion has no servers and no accounts of its own. Nothing leaves your computer except the calls to Google Calendar and to an AI provider that you choose to set up.

Google Calendar

Signing in with Google is optional. Work Companion asks only for read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly) so it can show today's events, alert you before meetings and ask for a note when one ends. It never changes your calendar.
